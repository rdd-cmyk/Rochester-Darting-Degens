Why these contrast rules?

Current orange with navy text: 5.56:1 light / 6.82:1 dark. White on light orange: 2.71:1, below the 4.5:1 ordinary-text target. Subtle panel borders are decorative; essential control boundaries use a stronger role. Calculations concern selected solid pairs and do not establish site compliance.

Use WCAG 2.2 text contrast and non-text contrast as the source standards.
